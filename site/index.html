When does the current Diablo season end?

Live countdowns, start dates and end dates for the Diablo II: Resurrected ladder, Project Diablo 2, Diablo III and Diablo IV. Official dates are shown plainly; anything estimated is marked with ~.

How end dates work: when a season's end has been officially announced it is shown as-is. Otherwise we show an estimate, marked ~ in orange: either a date reported but not yet confirmed (for example from in-game data), or the start date plus that game's typical season length. Estimates are never official. All times are shown in your local time zone.
